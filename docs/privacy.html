Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11 · WeMD · Page to Markdown (WeChat-ready)
EN: WeMD does not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your data. Anywhere. By anyone.
中文：WeMD 不收集、不传输、不存储、不共享您的任何数据——任何情况下、对任何一方。

1. What WeMD does / WeMD 做什么

EN WeMD converts the web page you are viewing into Markdown, entirely inside your browser, on your machine. Page content never leaves your browser — conversion is 100% local. The extension makes no analytics, telemetry, or tracking requests of any kind. The only network call is Pro license activation, which sends just your license key and email to wemd.zyx.pink for verification — never page content.

中文 WeMD 将您正在浏览的网页转换为 Markdown，全程在您的浏览器内、您的机器上完成。网页内容永不离开您的浏览器——转换 100% 本地。扩展不发出任何统计、遥测或追踪请求。唯一的网络调用是 Pro 许可证激活：仅将您的许可证密钥和邮箱发送至 wemd.zyx.pink 进行验证——绝不发送网页内容。

2. Data we collect / 我们收集的数据

EN None. No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking of any kind. Converted Markdown exists only in memory while the popup is open, until you copy or download it. The extension persists no content.

中文 无。无账号、无登录、无云同步、无广告、无统计、无任何跟踪。转换出的 Markdown 仅在弹窗打开期间存在于内存中，直到您复制或下载；扩展不持久化任何内容。

3. Permissions / 权限说明

EN

  • activeTab + scripting: to read and convert the page you explicitly act on (popup click / context menu). The extension cannot read any page you have not interacted with through WeMD.
  • contextMenus: to provide the right-click "download as Markdown" entries.
  • clipboardWrite / downloads: to save converted Markdown to your clipboard or disk when you click Copy / Download.

中文

  • activeTab + scripting：仅用于读取并转换您通过 WeMD 主动操作的页面（点击弹窗 / 右键菜单）。您未通过 WeMD 操作的页面，扩展无法读取。
  • contextMenus：提供右键"下载为 Markdown"菜单项。
  • clipboardWrite / downloads：在您点击复制 / 下载时，把转换结果写入剪贴板或磁盘。

4. Third-party libraries / 第三方库

EN The conversion pipeline bundles two open-source libraries locally: Mozilla Readability (Apache-2.0) and Turndown (MIT). They run offline in your browser and make no requests.

中文 转换管线本地打包了两个开源库：Mozilla Readability（Apache-2.0）与 Turndown（MIT），均在浏览器内离线运行、不发出任何请求。

5. Pro purchases (when available) / Pro 购买（推出后）

EN If you purchase a Pro license, the transaction and related data (name, email, payment details) are processed by our payment provider (Paddle) under their privacy policy. WeMD itself still collects nothing; your license key is stored locally in your browser.

中文 若您购买 Pro 许可，交易及相关数据（姓名、邮箱、支付信息）由我们的支付服务商（Paddle）按其隐私政策处理。WeMD 本身仍不收集任何数据；您的许可密钥仅存储在您的浏览器本地。

6. Changes / 变更

EN If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, and the extension's release notes will call it out.
中文 如本政策发生变更，上方日期将随之更新，并会在扩展发布说明中提示。
