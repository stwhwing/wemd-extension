EN WeMD is a browser extension that converts web pages (including WeChat official-account articles) into Markdown, entirely locally inside your browser. The free version covers single-page and selection conversion with copy/download and a token-savings panel. "WeMD Pro" adds batch conversion, custom templates, image bundling and smart promo-tail truncation when released.
